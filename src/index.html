About

I am a Computer Science student at FAST - National University of Computer and Emerging Sciences (NUCES), driven by a passion for building software that is both functional and meaningful. I enjoy transforming ideas into real-world applications through programming and constantly challenge myself with projects that strengthen my problem-solving and software development skills.

My primary interests lie in C++, web development, and object-oriented programming. I enjoy designing intuitive user interfaces, writing clean and maintainable code, and exploring how software can solve everyday problems. Whether I'm developing desktop applications, experimenting with modern web technologies, or learning new tools, I approach every project as an opportunity to grow as a developer.

Outside the classroom, I believe the best way to learn is by building. Every project I create helps me deepen my understanding of programming concepts while encouraging creativity, attention to detail, and continuous improvement. My goal is to become a software engineer who develops reliable, efficient, and user-focused applications that make a positive impact.
